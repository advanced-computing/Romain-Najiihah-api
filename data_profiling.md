## Five Takeaways from NYC OpenData's Film Permits Dataset
1. The majority of the permits issued for Shooting Permits, which accounted for 78% of the recorded events.
2. The the EnteredOn column contains 1,727 missing values, which may suggest a gap in administrative recordkeeping and may affect the accuracy of permit processing timeliens.
3. Film permits are concentrated mainly in Manhattan (51%) and Brooklyn (33%), indicating   that other boroughs don't have as much film activity.
4. The EventAgency column contains only one distinct value ("Mayor's Office of Media & Entertainment"), indicating that a single agency is responsible for overseeing and issuing all film permits in this dataset.
5. The StartDateTime column contains 315 missing values (2%), which may impact event scheduling.
